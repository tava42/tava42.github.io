This app is used by one person only: its author. It reads the author’s own Google Health data (sleep, heart rate, HRV, activity) with read-only access and stores it on a private home server that is not reachable from the internet. Selected daily summaries, never raw data or account identifiers, may be sent to an AI model to write personal reports. Data is not shared, sold or used for advertising. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
